# Russian spelling supplement: common words older Russian Hunspell dictionaries lack.
# Written by WordCraft contributors (MIT OR Apache-2.0); see ATTRIBUTION.md.
# One word per line: the base form, a TAB, then its declension, from which
# crates/proof/src/ru/extra.rs makes every form:
#   m     masculine noun ending in a consonant (маркетплейс, -а, -у, -ом, -е, -ы/-и, -ов/-ей…)
#   f     feminine noun in -а (инфографика, -и, -е, -у, -ой…; genitive plural without ending)
#   fia   feminine noun in -ия (интеграция, -ии, -ию, -ией, -ий…)
#   fs    feminine noun in -ь (соцсеть, -и, -ью, -ей, -ям…)
#   nie   neuter noun in -ие (согласие, -ия, -ию, -ием, -ий…)
#   no    neuter noun in -о (юрлицо, -а, -у, -ом, -е; genitive plural without ending)
#   adj   adjective or participle in -ый, -ий, -ой (бонусный, газоиспользующий)
#   ovat  verb in -овать with its conjugation and participles (колеровать, администрировать)
#   =     the forms themselves, space-separated (verbs, nouns with a fleeting vowel)
# A word alone on its line doesn't decline (онлайн, медиа). Capitalized words are names and match
# only when written with a capital.
