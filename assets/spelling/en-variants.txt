# English spelling variants: American and British forms of the same word, for the English
# dialects (#1281, #1045). Written by WordCraft contributors from general knowledge of English
# spelling (MIT OR Apache-2.0); no other word list copied. See ATTRIBUTION.md.

# Each line: POLICY AMERICAN BRITISH
# POLICY is three letters, for American English (en-US), Canadian English (en-CA) and the
# British-spelling Englishes (en-GB, en-AU, en-NZ, en-IE, en-ZA, en-IN…), in that order:
#   u  only the American form is right there (the British one is flagged, American suggested)
#   b  only the British form is right there
#   *  both are right
# Inflections (colours, centred, organising, organisation…) come from the rules in
# crates/proof/src/en_variants.rs for -or/-our, -er/-re and -ize/-ise style pairs; other pairs
# get only a plural, so list their other forms on lines of their own. When in doubt a form is
# accepted: a false alarm is worse than a missed variant.
