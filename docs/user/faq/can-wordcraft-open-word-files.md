Yes. WordCraft opens and saves .docx, the format Microsoft Word uses. Documents saved in WordCraft open in Word.

## Formats

- **Open and save:** .docx, Word XML Document (.xml), plus .odt, .rtf, .html, .md, .tex and .txt.
- **Open only:** Word 97-2003 .doc files and Word 2003 XML files. WordCraft has no .doc writer, so save as .docx.
- **Export only:** PDF and page images (.png). See [Export to PDF and other formats](../guides/export-to-pdf.md).

## Limits

WordCraft is in alpha. Its roadmap lists testing against real-world .docx files, and keeping charts and SmartArt through a round trip, as work needed for beta. Check an important document in both programs before you rely on the result.

Documents that ask for fonts such as Calibri or Cambria are shown with installed system fonts or open fonts of matching size, because WordCraft does not bundle those fonts.

To open a file, see [Open and save documents](../guides/open-and-save-documents.md).
