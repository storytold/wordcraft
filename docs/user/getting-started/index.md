Install WordCraft, open it, pick a template and start typing. Save with **Save** or **Save As** on the **File** tab.

## First steps

1. [Install WordCraft](install.md).
2. Start WordCraft. It opens on the **File** tab at **Home**, with a greeting, a row of templates and your recent documents.
3. Click a template: **Blank document**, **Studio handbook (sample)**, **Letter** or **Résumé**. Click **More templates →** to see them all, including **Report**, or use the **Search for templates** box on **New**.
4. Type. The ribbon has the tabs File, Home, Insert, Draw, Design, Layout, References, Mailings, Review, View, Developer, Zotero and Help. **Table Design** and **Table Layout** appear when the caret is in a table, **Picture Format** when a picture is selected and **Shape Format** when a shape is selected. Charts, SmartArt and equations bring up their own tabs too.
5. Open the **File** tab, click **Save As**, then **Browse…** to pick a name and place.

New blank documents follow your system's region for paper size and measurement units: for example Letter and inches in the US, A4 and centimetres in most other countries.

To start in an empty document instead of **Home**, click the **File** tab, then **Options**, and clear **Show the Start screen when WordCraft starts** under **General**.
