Put the caret where the table should go. Open **References**, click **Table of Contents** and pick an automatic table. WordCraft builds it from your headings.

## Steps

1. Give your chapter and section titles heading styles, such as **Heading 1**, from the Styles gallery on the **Home** tab.
2. Place the caret where the table should appear.
3. Click the **References** tab.
4. In the **Table of Contents** group, click **Table of Contents** and choose:
   - **Automatic Table 1 (3 levels)**, or
   - **Automatic Table 2 (2 levels)**, which adds the title "Table of Contents", or
   - **Custom Table of Contents…** to set the table up in a dialog.

## Update the table

After you change headings or pages, click **Update Table** in the same group.

## Add text to the table

In the **Table of Contents** group, **Add Text** offers **Do Not Show in TOC**, **Level 1**, **Level 2** or **Level 3**.

## Remove the table

Click **Table of Contents** and choose **Remove Table of Contents**.
