Write the letter, load a recipient list, insert merge fields, preview and click **Finish & Merge**. All the buttons are on the **Mailings** tab.

## Steps

1. Click the **Mailings** tab.
2. Click **Start Mail Merge** and choose a kind: **Letters**, **E-mail Messages**, **Envelopes**, **Labels**, **Directory** or **Normal Word Document**.
3. Click **Select Recipients**, then:
   - **Use an Existing List…** and pick a file. The picker offers .csv, .tsv and .txt files, and Excel (.xlsx, .xlsm) and OpenDocument (.ods) spreadsheets.
   - **Type a New List…** to enter the recipients by hand.
4. The first row of the list names the fields. Click **Edit Recipient List** to review it.
5. Click in the letter where a value belongs, then click **Insert Merge Field** and choose a field. **Address Block** and **Greeting Line** insert common groups of fields.
6. Click **Preview Results** to see the letter with real values. **Previous Record** and **Next Record** step through the recipients.
7. Click **Check for Errors** to look for problems.
8. Click **Finish & Merge**.

## What Finish & Merge does

It builds one merged document from your template and the recipient list, and that document replaces the one you have open. If you have unsaved changes, WordCraft first asks whether to save them. Save your template before you merge if you want to keep it.

## Other buttons

- **Highlight Merge Fields** shows where fields sit.
- **Rules** adds **If…Then…Else…**, **Merge Record #**, **Next Record** and **Skip Record If…**.
- **Match Fields** and **Find Recipient** help with the list.
- **Envelopes** and **Labels** in the **Create** group open a dialog to set up those documents. **New Document** replaces the open document. In the **Envelopes** dialog, **Add to Document** puts the envelope into your document instead.
