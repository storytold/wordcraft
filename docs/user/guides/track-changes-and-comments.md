Open the **Review** tab and click **Track Changes**. Your edits are then marked, and you or a reviewer can accept or reject each one.

## Turn on Track Changes

1. Click the **Review** tab.
2. In the **Tracking** group, click **Track Changes**. The button shows a check mark while tracking is on.
3. Edit the document. Insertions and deletions show as markup. The keyboard shortcut is Ctrl+Shift+E (⌘+Shift+E on macOS).

In the same group, **Display for Review** switches between **All Markup** and **No Markup**. **Reviewing Pane** lists the changes.

## Accept or reject changes

1. Click the change.
2. In the **Changes** group, click **Accept** and choose **Accept This Change** or **Accept All Changes**. Or click **Reject** and choose **Reject Change** or **Reject All Changes**.
3. Use **Previous** and **Next** in the **Changes** group to move from change to change.

## Add a comment

1. Select the text to comment on.
2. In the **Comments** group, click **New Comment**.
3. Type the comment.

In the same group, **Previous** and **Next** move between comments, **Delete** removes the current one and **Show Comments** switches the comments pane on or off. **Resolve** and **Reopen** are on the comment itself (in the balloon or the comments pane), not in the ribbon group.
